Compass Pro Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Compass Pro (“the app”) is published by Boss Productions (“we”). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The app has no accounts and we do not run a server that stores your personal data.

Location

The app uses your device’s precise and approximate location to show coordinates, altitude, heading, weather and your position on a map. Location is used only while the app is open. The app does not access location in the background.

Sensors, camera and torch

The app reads the magnetometer, accelerometer and other motion sensors to work as a compass and level. The torch feature switches on your device flashlight. Sensor readings are processed on your device and are not sent to us.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, saved locations, tracking records and emergency contacts are stored on your device. We do not receive them. Uninstalling the app or using Clear Cache in Settings removes this data.

Third-party services

  • Open-Meteo receives approximate coordinates to return weather data.
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers receive map tile requests, which reveal your IP address and the area you view.
  • Google AdMob shows ads. It may collect your advertising ID, IP address and device information to serve and measure ads. See Google’s privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • RevenueCat and Google Play process subscriptions and purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase information. We never see your payment details.
  • Firebase (Google) may collect app diagnostics such as crash reports and basic usage data.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the services listed above, to run the app.

Retention and deletion

Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. To ask about or delete data held by our service providers (for example purchase records), email us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
